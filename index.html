§01 Profile

I'm a cybersecurity student with a standing interest in how networks actually behave, not just how the diagram says they should. Most of my time goes into penetration testing practice and open-source intelligence gathering: tracing what's exposed, what's discoverable, and what a system reveals about itself without meaning to.

Day to day, that runs through Arch Linux with a Hyprland setup tuned for speed over decoration. Code and writeups both live on github.com/corvainx.
